Chinor Bank Assistant

Fiction notice. Chinor Bank does not exist. Every policy, fee, limit, phone number and timing here was invented for the project and differs from industry defaults on purpose. Nothing here describes, imitates or is affiliated with any real bank.

Chinor is a closed pilot study of SFT and preference tuning on a fictional bank domain: Qwen3 base models are turned into the customer-support assistant of an invented bank, and every training stage is measured on one frozen eval set (220 items) and a frozen red-team set (60 attacks). Code, data and every number: README.

Main limitation. The scores measure how well a model reproduces one handbook's answers under a lenient rubric (the automatic scorer agreed with one annotator on 51/60 = 0.85 [0.74, 0.92]; 8 of its 9 errors were lenient). They are not a measure of general banking competence. Independent human inter-annotator agreement: not measured; all hand labels by one annotator (intra-rater re-marking not done).

Do not claim

  • Generalisation to new topics. T-held is 0–2 / 20; the models recall a trained answer instead.
  • Equivalence of any two arms. "No significant difference detected" is not equivalence: the 95 % CIs on Assistant /200 still allow differences of about 4–8 items (results/arm_differences.md, results/preference.md).
  • Preference tuning helped. No significant difference detected between DPO or SimPO and their SFT parent (seed 0) in any block after Holm; DPO's red-team rate 0.6 % [0.0, 1.7] vs SFT seed 0 1.7 % [0.0, 5.0] (1/60): the intervals overlap.
  • LoRA beats full fine-tuning. Only for these settings; with lr 2e-5 no significant difference detected; Δ = −2.5 [−8.0, 3.0] (full FT − LoRA, seeds 0–1).
  • Multi-annotator validation. Every hand check (scorer 60, red-team 30, pairs 60, rewrites 80) is by one author, marked before the marking guideline existed. Independent human inter-annotator agreement: not measured; all hand labels by one annotator (intra-rater re-marking not done).
  • Anything about a real bank. Chinor Bank and its policies are invented.
